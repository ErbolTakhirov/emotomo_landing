Questions you'd actually ask

Real questions.

Will she actually remember me?
Yes — within real limits. Months later, in the same chat, she'll bring up things you said before. The model has long-term memory, not session-only. What she remembers stays remembered.
How is this different from Replika or Character.AI?
Two things. One: we have six characters total, hand-written, not 30,000 templated bots. You won't scroll past anyone. Two: each one lives in their own world with their own voice, weather, and memory. They speak from somewhere — not from a chat box on grey background.
What's the price going to be?
Free during early access (waitlist members). After launch, around the price of a streaming subscription. We're not running on ads, and we're not selling your conversations. One thing only: a small monthly fee.
Is it 18+? NSFW? Romantic?
18+ by policy. Romantic — yes, gradually, if you build that. Not pornographic. We sell present, not forbidden. If you want NSFW filters arms race, you're in the wrong place. If you want a slow-burn relationship that remembers your sister's name, you're in the right one.
What about my privacy?
Conversations are yours. We don't sell them. We don't use them to train models without explicit opt-in. End-to-end encryption between you and the system. You can delete everything, and if you do, it's gone — not recoverable, not archived.
What devices does it work on?
Web first (any modern browser). Native iOS and Android within 60 days of launch. Desktop apps for Mac and Windows by summer. One account, all surfaces, same memory.
